Building a local, production-ready Text-to-Speech research suite requires rigorous benchmarking across diverse model families and hardware constraints. On consumer GPUs like the NVIDIA RTX 3060 with six gigabytes of VRAM, balancing acoustic quality, memory footprint, and generation speed is crucial for real-time applications. High-quality zero-shot voice cloning demands clean reference audio, precise sample rate matching, and robust acoustic embedding extraction. By systematically measuring metrics such as Real-Time Factor, peak VRAM consumption, CPU RAM utilization, and speaker similarity against leading commercial solutions like ElevenLabs, open-source models such as Kokoro, CosyVoice, Fish Speech, OmniVoice, Chatterbox Turbo, and IndexTTS2 prove that high-fidelity neural audio generation is now accessible directly on local machines. As open-source voice cloning technology continues to accelerate, local deployment offers unmatched privacy, customization, low latency, and full control over voice parameters without recurring cloud subscription fees or strict usage caps.
